# Academic Literature Review: 7 Core Table Blueprints

This reference guide provides ready-to-use table structures and data schemas for systematically reviewing empirical, managerial, and policy literature.

---

### Table 1: Practice / Technology Taxonomy & Input Dynamics
* **Goal**: Shift literature definition from abstract ecological/social goals to concrete managerial choices and variable cost implications.
* **Schema**:
  - `Practice / Category`: Name of practice (e.g., Conservation Tillage, Cover Cropping, Precision Nitrogen).
  - `Input Allocation & Operational Nature`: Specific machinery, seed, labor, fuel, and chemical changes required.
  - `Economic Transformation & Asset Dynamics`: Immediate cash outlays vs. multi-year soil capital appreciation.
  - `Empirical Literature Grounding`: Key peer-reviewed citations.

---

### Table 2: Boolean Search Architecture (PRISMA 2020)
* **Goal**: Provide an audit trail reproducible in Web of Science, Scopus, or PubMed.
* **Schema**:
  - `Conceptual Block`: Core research concept (e.g., Practice, Geography, Decision Agents, Microeconomic Mechanisms).
  - `Boolean Query Formulation`: Exact search terms with wildcards (`*`) and boolean operators (`OR`, `AND`).

---

### Table 3: Audited Screening Funnel (Record Attrition)
* **Goal**: Fulfill PRISMA 2020 reporting standards with verified counts.
* **Schema**:
  - `Screening Stage`: Identification, Deduplication, Eligibility Screening, Included Synthesis Pool.
  - `Record Volume`: Exact counts ($N$ values).
  - `Methodological Documentation & Attrition Criteria`: Specific inclusion/exclusion rules applied.

---

### Table 4: Thematic Empirical Synthesis Matrix
* **Goal**: Group the body of reviewed literature by empirical themes and present quantified findings.
* **Schema**:
  - `Thematic Pillar`: Thematic category (e.g., Yield Penalties, Cash Flow Payback, Soil Moisture Retention).
  - `Included Studies (N)`: Number of empirical studies addressing this pillar.
  - `Empirical Metrics & Quantified Ranges`: Key numerical findings (e.g., "1.0% to 2.2% yield penalty/day", "3 to 5 year payback").
  - `Representative Citations`: Landmark papers supporting the metrics.

---

### Table 5: Institutional, Contractual & Microeconomic Frictions vs. Solutions
* **Goal**: Cross-examine real-world bottlenecks against market/policy mechanisms.
* **Schema**:
  - `Friction Category`: Short-Term Cash Flow Deficit, Planting Schedule Bottleneck, 1-Year Cash Lease Moral Hazard, etc.
  - `Empirical Bottleneck`: Why rational operators resist or hesitate (cost shocks, risk exposure, asset asymmetry).
  - `Market & Contractual Solutions`: Green leases, risk-sharing contracts, insurance discounts, flexible planting green protocols.
  - `Empirical Literature Support`: Citations providing field validation.

---

### Table 6: Empirical Measurement & Survey Parameter Mapping
* **Goal**: Directly link literature review findings to downstream empirical instruments (e.g., farmer surveys, Discrete Choice Experiments).
* **Schema**:
  - `Construct / Dimension`: Latent variable or decision attribute.
  - `Survey Question / Conjoint Attribute`: Specific operationalization (e.g., "Planting Delay Tolerance (Days)", "Lease Duration (1 vs 3 vs 5 years)").
  - `Operational Scale / Levels`: Response choices or experimental levels.
  - `Theoretical & Empirical Precedent`: Citations justifying the scale design.

---

### Table 7: Research Scope, Phases & Boundary Conditions
* **Goal**: Position the review within the broader research project.
* **Schema**:
  - `Research Phase`: Phase 1 (Systematic Review), Phase 2 (Producer Survey), Phase 3 (Industry Strategic Report).
  - `Core Objective`: Primary research question addressed.
  - `Analytical Methodology`: Systematic synthesis, econometric/conjoint modeling, macroeconomic scenario simulation.
  - `Deliverable`: Academic paper, survey instrument, executive strategy whitepaper.
